Our Story & Philosophy

Crafted for Slow Mornings & Warm Conversations.

Born from a love for neighborhood gathering spaces and precision roasting, KJS Roasters began with a simple mission: to honor every farmer, bean, and guest who walks through our doors. Whether you're settling in with a novel or catching up with old friends, our space is designed to feel like home.

Quality Beans

Direct-trade, high-altitude single-origin harvests roasted weekly in-house.

Fresh Ingredients

Organic pasture-raised dairy, pure Madagascar vanilla, and locally milled flour.

Cozy Sanctuary

Sunlit seating, acoustic vinyl playlists, and welcoming hospitality.
